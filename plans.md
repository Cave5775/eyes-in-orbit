# Feature issues
- [x] Surface view centers on the point between the surface and the object. Change to camera to be focused on the object but oriented with the surface. Make this the enabled by default when viewing an object in orbit, suborbit, or ground.
  - Fixed: the surface view is now centred on the object itself (no midpoint lift; `surfaceLift`/`surfaceFrame`/`groundPoint` are gone) and keeps the local vertical up, north-up azimuth and level horizon. The fit distance frames a sphere as tall as the object's height (`surfaceFitDistance`: ISS about 1,400 km, a rocket at +5 min about 500 km, 20 km minimum for pads and boosters near the ground, 60,000 km at most, so at GEO the ground leaves the frame), and the camera may orbit below a high object's height (`minElevation` takes the target altitude). It is on by default: `focus()` ends in `applyAutoSurface`, which uses the pure `shouldAutoSurface` (`lib/surface.ts`, tested) to switch with the usual blend for any entity of the Earth whose altitude is under 100,000 km (orbit, ascent, booster, ground station, pad entity) and for pads focused by lat/lon, and to go back to the globe view for deep-space objects, entities of other bodies and while the Moon, the Sun or another non-Earth body is the frame. S or SURFACE off is remembered in `localStorage` (`eio.surface`), nothing re-enables it until S is pressed again (restores the default); Esc, FIT of a group and the Moon/Sun tabs leave the view without changing that choice. Decisions: the altitude cut is 100,000 km (not the 2e6 km shell threshold, which would include the Moon's distance); deselecting an object leaves the surface view for the globe (it used to freeze a point in space); right/shift drag releases the follow and pins the target to where the object was, at its height, then slides it along the sphere at that height; internal refocuses (FIT in the surface view, MODEL) do not trigger the policy. Checked in a headless browser with the mock catalogue (ISS, a launch vehicle at +5 min, a booster, a ground station and a pad entity, JWST/Voyager staying in the globe view, S off then select, S on, pan, FIT, deselect, Moon frame); not checked: real catalogue data, touch, high GPU load, or a pad focused by lat/lon (the mock launches all have entities).
