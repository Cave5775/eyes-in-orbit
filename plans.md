## Issues
- [x] Camera dips down when transitioning to surface mode
  - Fixed in code, mock mode only (`blendPosition` in `lib/camera.ts`, `OrbitRig.switchMode`): the old blend interpolated the target, the offset direction and the offset length separately, so the camera swung through the planet and then sat on the 3 km floor for tens of frames. The camera now flies round the Earth between its start position and the surface pose, never below the lower of its start and end elevations nor `MIN_ALT_KM`. Measured over 20 cases (ISS, launch vehicle, booster, ground station from 5 globe poses): dip below the horizon up to 22.7 degrees before, 0.0 after; minimum altitude 3 km before, the end altitude after. The same blend applies to the S-off and Esc transitions back to the globe. Side effect: from a far-side start the object stays behind the planet for part of the blend. Not checked: a real GPU, touch. Seen but not investigated: Esc in the middle of an unfinished blend can leave the free target inside the Earth.
